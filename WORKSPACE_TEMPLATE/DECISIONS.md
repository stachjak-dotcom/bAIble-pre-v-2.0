## Decision rule

A material project rule must not be silently inferred from an underspecified task.

If the project has not decided the rule:
- record the question as OPEN or PROPOSED;
- record the relevant alternatives;
- identify who or what has authority to decide;
- do not present an implementation choice as the project's authoritative specification.

This template does not define who has authority. Authority remains project-specific.

A decision may later be revised when scope changes or new evidence contradicts it.
