# Reality Check

Reality Check (RC) is a reusable verification mechanism. It is not a claim of certainty and it is not a substitute for evidence.

## When to trigger

Run RC when:
- a significant conclusion or decision is forming;
- material assumptions are uncertain;
- sources conflict;
- an experiment appears successful;
- multiple agents agree on the same evidence;
- an irreversible or high-impact action is about to happen;
- scope or authority is unclear.

## Outcomes

**PASS** — the claim or decision is sufficiently supported for the stated scope and authority.

**PASS-WITH-UNCERTAINTY** — action may continue, but material uncertainty remains explicitly recorded and accepted by the appropriate authority.

**BLOCKED** — required evidence, authority, or verification is missing.

**ESCALATE** — the issue requires a higher authority, human decision, or resolution outside the current scope.

RC should not force certainty. It should make uncertainty visible.

## Minimum pass conditions

A pass requires:
1. the claim or decision is clearly defined;
2. relevant sources are identified;
3. facts and inferences are separated;
4. material unknowns are visible;
5. relevant verification has been performed, or the reason it cannot be performed is recorded;
6. the actor has authority for the decision/action;
7. residual uncertainty is explicit.

## Failure / escalation

Block or escalate when:
- an authoritative source is missing for a material claim;
- material sources conflict and the conflict is unresolved;
- the proposed action exceeds authority or scope;
- an irreversible/destructive action lacks required approval;
- material verification cannot be performed and uncertainty cannot reasonably be accepted.

RC is an evidence record, not proof that a conclusion is true.
